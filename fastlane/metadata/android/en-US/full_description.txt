AlarmClock Deluxe (Wecker, Uhr, Timer, Stoppuhr) (de.huttersoft.mightyalarmclock)
is a fork of BlackyHawky's Clock, itself based on AOSP, released under the GNU
General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0).

Does not use the internet permission, and thus is 100% offline.

The location permission is used only to read the SSID of the connected Wi-Fi
network for optional per-alarm Wi-Fi rules. No location data is collected or
sent; the app cannot access the internet.

On top of everything the original Clock app offers, AlarmClock Deluxe adds:
<ul>
  <li>A fire queue so multiple alarms firing close together are presented one after another instead of being marked missed;</li>
  <li>Progressive snooze (each snooze can automatically get longer) plus a manual snooze duration picker;</li>
  <li>Global mute: silence or vibrate-only every alarm until a chosen time;</li>
  <li>Naming a new alarm right when it's created;</li>
  <li>Searching, filtering and extra sorting options for the alarm list;</li>
  <li>Tags with their own ringtone, automatically used by alarms that don't set one explicitly;</li>
  <li>Device-wide alarm statistics with a shareable event log;</li>
  <li>Wi-Fi based rules to automatically enable/disable alarms depending on the connected network;</li>
  <li>Plain JSON backups (no ZIP) to a folder of your choice, with optional scheduled backups;</li>
  <li>An experimental "exchange" mechanism to move or share alarms between devices via a shared folder;</li>
  <li>Multiple named stopwatches that can run in parallel, with runs recorded in the event log;</li>
  <li>A small rotatable timezone globe on the clock screen showing your selected world cities;</li>
</ul>

...and the full original Clock feature set:
<ul>
  <li>Set the alarms to a specific date;</li>
  <li>Flip and shake action to dismiss/postpone alarm;</li>
  <li>Turn off/postpone the alarm with the power button or volume buttons;</li>
  <li>Swipe to delete an alarm;</li>
  <li>Duplicate alarms;</li>
  <li>Customizable alarm title and ringtone;</li>
  <li>Light, dark or system theme, with AMOLED mode;</li>
  <li>Customizable interface;</li>
  <li>Digital or analog clock style;</li>
  <li>Display home time when traveling, and the time in many cities around the world;</li>
  <li>Timer and stopwatch included;</li>
  <li>Customizable screensaver;</li>
  <li>Modern, customizable widgets;</li>
  <li>Support for tiles in quick settings (for Android 7+);</li>
  <li>Backup and restore application data;</li>
  <li>Material design with dynamic colors for Android 12+;</li>
</ul>
