First public CalVer release (2026.08.001) of AlarmClock Deluxe.

Fork of BlackyHawky's Clock with richer alarm management: fire queue,
progressive snooze, global mute, tags, search/filters, Wi-Fi rules,
JSON backups, Syncthing-friendly exchange, multi-stopwatch, timezone
globe, and more. Location permission is used only to read the Wi-Fi SSID
for optional rules — no internet access.
